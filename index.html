How it works

Built for trust, not friction.

Identity verification should protect users without making online transactions feel invasive or overwhelming.

◎

Identity proofing

Verify identity by combining trusted credentials, step-up checks, and risk-aware decision rules.

△

Secure matching

Use privacy-preserving matching methods and strong authentication to reduce fraud without over-collecting data.

◈

Transaction approval

Confirm risk thresholds, support step-up verification, and create an auditable transaction record.

Privacy by design

Protect identity data with clear boundaries.

Any serious biometric or identity verification system should be transparent about purpose, retention, consent, legal basis, and user control.

▣

Data minimization

Collect only the specific identity attributes needed for the transaction and avoid excessive retention.

⟡

Consent-first UX

Give users clear explanations, control over sharing, and easy ways to review or withdraw consent.

⎈

Retention controls

Set tight retention periods, deletion workflows, and secure access policies for sensitive records.

Biometric data demands exceptional care.

When used, biometrics should be encrypted, access-limited, and subject to strict governance and legal review.
